# Content Quality Findings — saharasoft.org

**Score: 55/100 (weakest category)** · 10 pages read in full, 2026-08-28

## Strengths
1. Consistent, verifiable NAP (+254 721 137000, Nairobi) + ProfessionalService schema with geo on every page
2. Real product evidence: 10 named products on live domains (tallydue.com, getbaylink.com, inbuildr.com, arbecapp…)
3. Locally specific, quotable lines: "M-Pesa paybill or till… usually non-negotiable for real businesses"; "The gap between reading a meter and collecting the money is where revenue disappears"
4. Clean structure: 1 H1/page, dated `<time>` posts, Challenge/Solution/Outcome case-study box, "Related reading" blocks
5. Real internal-link cluster: water post ↔ TallyDue case study ↔ mobile-money post ↔ pricing

## Weaknesses (with evidence)
1. **No human authorship** — bylines "By Saharasoft Solutions"; team grid: "Nelson — Founder & Lead Engineer" (no surname/LinkedIn), anonymous "Engineering"/"Product Design" cards with SVG avatars (about.html)
2. **Cost post answers the cost query with zero numbers** — section "What projects cost in Kenya" says "There is no honest 'starting from' number" while pricing.html promises "the ranges Kenyan projects typically fall into" in that exact post → broken internal promise
3. **Anonymized, metric-free flagship case study** — "Client: A water utility in Kenya"; only metric "closes billing cycles in days instead of weeks", reused verbatim on homepage; no client name/scale/KES/quote/timeframe
4. **Thin money pages** — services ~355 words for "custom software development services" head term (competitors 1,000+); why-invest post ~269 generic words, only post with no contextual internal links
5. **Homepage = products page, tile-for-tile** — identical 10-tile grid (same H3s/copy/images); homepage also reuses TallyDue case-study text nearly verbatim

## Cannibalization pairs
1. index.html ↔ products/index.html (identical product grid)
2. index.html ↔ software-development-services.html (both funnel "custom software development" head intent)
3. pricing.html ↔ blog/custom-software-cost-kenya.html (same query; no numbers on either)

## Citability
Positioning is quotable, but site-wide the only figures are unsubstantiated "90+/8+/12+" and "days instead of weeks". No FAQ sections anywhere. AI engines have almost nothing concrete to cite for the exact queries these pages target.
