Case study — Construction & Field Ops

Inbuildr connects field crews and office teams with real-time task tracking

Product: Inbuildr

In construction and field operations, the office and the site usually live in different worlds. Task status travelled by phone call, and by the time it reached the office, it was already stale.

The challenge

Crews on site completed work that the office only learned about days later. Progress reports were assembled manually, so project visibility lagged reality. When something slipped — materials, a contractor, a deadline — it was discovered late, when the delay had already compounded.

The solution

We built Inbuildr, a project and operations tracking platform for construction and field teams. Tasks are assigned, updated, and reported from the field in real time, and the office sees the same picture.

  • Project and task tracking with clear ownership and deadlines.
  • Field updates captured on mobile, where the work happens.
  • Real-time status visibility for office teams and management.
  • Reports that assemble themselves from field data.
  • Notifications that keep both sides of the project aligned.

The outcome

Field crews and office teams work from the same real-time picture. Project visibility improves, delays surface while they can still be corrected, and reporting stops consuming the office's week.
