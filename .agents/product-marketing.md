## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Custom software built for how African businesses actually work.
**What it does:** Saharasoft Solutions designs, builds, and runs custom web and mobile products for businesses whose workflows don't fit off-the-shelf tools — from water utility billing to logistics, events, real estate, and internal operations. It also operates a portfolio of products (TallyDue, Arbec, Inbuildr, Baylink, and others) built on the same principle.
**Product category:** Custom software development agency / product partner (B2B services).
**Product type:** Service business (custom development, product design, system integration, maintenance/retainers) + owned SaaS products.
**Business model:** Engagement tiers — fixed-fee discovery & consultation; milestone-based custom projects; monthly retainer & ongoing support. Products also available as operated services or customizable.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** African businesses — water utilities, real-estate firms, logistics operators, event/conference organisers, construction & field-service firms, healthcare operations, fleet/garage operators — plus clients in the UK, US, and Europe who want Africa-aware product teams.
**Decision-makers:** Founders/owners, operations directors, CTOs/technical leads, utility and municipal managers.
**Primary use case:** Replacing manual or mismatched processes (spreadsheets, paper, WhatsApp chains, generic SaaS) with software built around the real workflow.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Get a system that fits our actual workflow instead of adapting to a generic product.
- Collect payments/dues faster (e.g., utility billing in days not weeks).
- Get field and office teams working from the same real-time picture.
**Use cases:** water billing & meter reading; conference management; construction/field project tracking; logistics dispatch & tracking; property/rent management; internal operations tools.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Off-the-shelf tools and manual processes don't match how African businesses actually operate (mobile-first users, intermittent connectivity, M-Pesa payments, utility billing cycles, logistics realities).
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Generic SaaS: per-user fees, no local payment/mobile context, features you pay for and never use.
- Foreign templates/agencies: built for other markets' workflows, resold without adaptation.
- Manual/spreadsheet processes: lost readings, late bills, invisible arrears, stale project status.
**What it costs them:** weeks of billing cycle time, revenue leakage, missed deadlines, duplicated data entry.
**Emotional tension:** "We know the process is broken, but we've been burned by software that doesn't fit."

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other African custom dev agencies / freelancers — vary wildly in process and follow-through; few have 12+ years of shipped, operating products.
**Secondary:** Off-the-shelf SaaS (billing, project management, event tools) — generic, no local context, subscription lock-in.
**Indirect:** Continuing with spreadsheets/paper/WhatsApp — familiar but unscalable.
**How each falls short:** see Problems & Pain Points.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Billing/ops cycles that take weeks; manual reconciliation; per-user SaaS costs; software that ignores local payments.
**Pull:** Built-for-Africa positioning, visible proof, ownership, milestone pricing, "reply with the clearest next step even if not hiring us".

**Anxiety:** Cost uncertainty, fear of another failed build, losing control to a vendor.
